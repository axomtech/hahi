Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Information We Collect

The booking enquiry form on this website collects the following details when you choose to fill it in:

  • Your name
  • Your contact number (optional)
  • Pickup location and destination
  • Travel date and return date (optional)
  • Number of passengers
  • Vehicle preference and trip type
  • Additional requirements (optional)

How Your Information Is Used

  • The information you enter in the booking form is used solely to prepare a booking enquiry message.
  • When you submit the form, the message opens in WhatsApp for you to review and send. You choose whether to send it.
  • This static website does not use a database and does not intentionally save your booking form data on its own servers.

WhatsApp Is a Third-Party Service

  • WhatsApp is an external service operated by Meta Platforms, Inc., with its own privacy practices and terms.
  • Once you send a message through WhatsApp, the information is handled according to WhatsApp's privacy policy and terms of service.
  • We encourage you to review WhatsApp's privacy policy to understand how your data is treated on that platform.

Cookies and Analytics

  • This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or unnecessary cookies by default.
  • If analytics, cookies, or advertising tools are introduced in the future, they will be clearly disclosed on this page before or at the time of introduction.
